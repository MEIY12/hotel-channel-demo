## Tips for Using This Content

### Content Reuse Strategies

Each property above demonstrates how the same fields can tell very different stories:

- **Room descriptions** emphasize different features (views, technology, history)
- **Amenities** reflect the property's focus (ski access, business center, historic charm)
- **Neighborhood vibe** connects to different traveler interests
- **Signature experiences** showcase unique value propositions

### Creating Your Own Entries

When creating additional entries, consider:

1. **Target Audience:** Who is this property for?
2. **Unique Selling Point:** What makes it different?
3. **Location Context:** How does the neighborhood enhance the stay?
4. **Channel Variations:** How will this content work in corporate vs. luxury views?

### Image Suggestions

For each property type, search for:

- **Urban Boutique:** Modern hotel rooms with city views
- **Beach Resort:** Ocean view rooms, infinity pools, coastal landscapes
- **Mountain Lodge:** Ski resort interiors, mountain vistas, fireplaces
- **Business Hotel:** Executive suites, meeting spaces, work areas
- **Historic Boutique:** Period architecture, antique furnishings, gardens
